How to read the engine

Breshears' thesis is simple and strange: the Great Pyramid encodes a calendar of catastrophe and prophecy. Fixed events — a comet in 1135 BC, the Great Flood at 2239 BC — act as anchors, and precise spans of years measured from them land on later landmarks: quakes, wars, the birth of the A.D. system, and events still ahead.

The numbers recur. A base cycle of 552 years doubles to 1104; the whole grid rests on 138 ( 552 = 138×4, 1104 = 138×8 ). Chart II·b mirrors 1656 years on either side of the Flood — a chronological palindrome. The ✦ interactives below rebuild these as living diagrams — and the Constellation cross-map shows how they all connect; the other plates are the original hand-drawn charts, zoomable and pristine.
