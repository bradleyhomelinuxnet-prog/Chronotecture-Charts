Triangle

* "1828 P″" (vertical from the entry)
* "1804 AD is 302 years to Armageddon" (arrow to the triangle's lower corner)
* "1828 P″ to exact depth level with floor joint. 3870 AM at entry becomes 5698 AM, or 1804 AD, exactly 302 years to 6000 Annus Mundi [2106 AD]. This ENDS Giza Countdown, but completed triangle brings us back to 1902 AD floor joint 302 P″ from central axis 1902 AD Great Step, the BEGINNING of the Giza Course Countdown."

Structures drawn without labels

* Upper Chamber with relieving chambers, Grand Gallery, Middle Chamber and its passage, ascending passage, Lower Chamber (dashed outline below the floor joint)

Reading notes

* "[25 BC]" is clear on the plate and agrees with the AM count used throughout (3895 − 25 = 3870).
* In "ENDS" and "BEGINNING" the plate underlines the words; rendered in capitals here.
