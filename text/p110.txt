Plate 110 · Foundation of Time · transcription of the drawn plate
The 7344-year timeline on the pyramid’s sloping corners — 8640, 1080 and 10,800

Top left

* 864 IS FOUNDATION OF TIME NUMBER, DENOMINATOR LINKING SECONDS, MINUTES, HOURS, DAYS, WEEKS, MONTHS AND YEARS

Top right

* EXTERIOR SLOPING DISTANCE OF GREAT PYRAMID'S 7344 P″ CONFIRMS MONUMENT'S INTERIOR 7344 YEAR TIMELINE AS DEMONSTRATED IN PYRAMIS CHRONICON AT END OF BOOK

On the pyramid

* 8640 [FOUNDATION OF TIME] (left corner edge)
* 7344 YEARS OF PYRAMID'S INTERNAL CHRONOLOGY (dashed line)
* SLOPING FACE AT MID-PLANE (dashed line)
* SLOPING CORNER ANGLE (right edge)
* 1080 YEARS (×4, along the base slab)

Left of pyramid

* AS THE MIDPOINT OF EACH FACE IS SLIGHTLY CONCAVE, THE PYRAMID ACTUALLY HAS 8 FACES. 8640÷8 IS 1080, OR 2815 BC (1080 AM), THE YEAR THE GREAT PYRAMID WAS FINISHED

Right of pyramid

* 864×4 CORNERS IS 3456.
* 3456 + 7344 IS 10,800
