Smyth now viewed the edifice in a new light and sought to show that the passage and chamber system had encoded within their dimensions a calendar of the past, present and future by isolating individual year-dates in its planes, joints, angles and passage lengths. (Prophets of Doom 93-94) After a thorough examination of the building he discovered that the Pyramid Inch was precisely 1.001 of the British inch, and that the entire monument was measured out in all of its dimensions using this standard of measurement. (The GReat Pyramid: Its Secrets and Mysteries Revealed: Smyth 1880 (W. Isbister, London, reprinted 1978 BE11 Pub. Co. NY) intro.) Smyth even found ancient evidence in the existence of the Pyramid Inch in the fact that the Greek word for pyramid derived from the older Phoenician term for the structure which literally meant light-measures. And as we will discover in this work, the Pyramid Inch is truly a fraction of the speed of light, each inch also representing the [?] orbital cycle of the earth around the sun, the principle source of all light in the solar sytem. Throughtout his work Smyth's chief aim was to demonstrate that the Great Pyramid, though standing silently in Egypt, was never of or belonging to the civilization we refer to as Egypt today. (The Great Pyramid: Smyth ix)

While Smyth was preparing for the release of his extensive research, an American had something rather remarkable published in 1879. Charles Latimer, Chief Engineer of the Railway in Cleveland, Ohio wrote that the Great Pyramid was a pillar containing the weights and measures of the ancient world and was inspired by God, and that the United States was the inheritor of the secrets of the monument as depicted by its Great Seal which has an image of the Great Pyramid. Mr. Latimer's article was published in Smyth's work, and the engineer wrote, "When a man or nation adopts a seal, it is understood that the most significant of all emblems, for them, is placed thereon. We look to the seal to give us the clue to the origin or genealogy of the wearer or owner...What other nation has such a seal? Will the Great Pyramid speak to those whose measures are not there? The Pyramid speaks in inches..." (The Great Pyramid: Smyth 252-253) More on the Great Seal later in this book.

The following year Piazzi Smyth's book was released in 1880, Our Inheritance in the Great Pyramid, an instant success depicting measurements throughout the structure as various timelines linking biblical and secular history. He dedicated the massive book to his friend John Taylor and his final published work served to prove Robert Menzies correct as well. The work was a success and pyramidology was virtually a new science with Piazzi as its priest. THough the calendrical variances between historical events changed little from Taylor's to Smyth's work, their timelines relied too heavily on Ussher's dating. Ussher's fictitious chronology would later prove their undoing.

Smyth received a professorship at Edinburge College, and the fact that such an eminent personage in the scientific community had released such inspiring and incredible claims actually offended many in the Royal Society. They searched for a champion to rebut these extraordinary claims and found one in the person of Sir William Flinder Petrie, a renowned archeologist having travelled around the world studying the ruins of lost civilizations. Petrie was an intellectual prodigy "...whose archeological survey methods first laid the basis for modern scientific archeological exploration, and whose experience in previous geodetic and other survey work eminently fitted him for the task of surveying the Great Pyramid." (The Great Pyramid: Its Divine Message 147)
