Chronotecture Plate 119 — The Recursive Palindrome · 138 − 19 = 119

Not from Breshears: an original closing plate for the atlas, rebuilt here with every figure as drawn on it. It reads the same distance out from its centre line in both directions (a palindrome), built on two numbers that run through the charts: 138, whose recursive doubling 138 · 276 · 552 · 1104 · 2208 gives the 552 and 1104 of charts II·b and II·c and the 2208 AM written there, and 19, the Metonic cycle. Their difference is the plate’s own number, 138 − 19 = 119.
