Plate 119 — The Recursive Palindrome

118  +  1  =  119  =  138 − 19

Not from Breshears — an original closing plate for the atlas. It reads the same distance out from the centre in both directions (a palindrome), built on the two numbers that quietly run through the whole work: 138, whose recursive doubling (138 · 276 · 552 · 1104 · 2208) supplies the cycles in charts II·b and II·c, and 19, the Metonic year-cycle. Their difference, 138 − 19 = 119, is the plate you asked for.

An original addition · numbers 19 & 138 · recursive & palindromic by construction.
