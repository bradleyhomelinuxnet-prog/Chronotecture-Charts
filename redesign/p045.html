Chronotecture The 52-year lattice around the 1356 AD casing-stone earthquake

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn chart XI·b (2007). Every date, span and annotation is kept verbatim; only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: 1356 AD [5250 AM] is the arrow, the year an earthquake let Muslims strip the Great Pyramid's casing blocks. Bars ending on it measure 104 to 780 years, nearly all multiples of 52, and bars from it reach 1656, 1701, 1876, 1908 and 2046 AD.
