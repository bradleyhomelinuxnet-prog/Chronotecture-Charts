Chronotecture 2045 CE, Chief Calendrical Theme of the Great Pyramid: the Lower Chamber Level

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn chart IX (2006). Every date, span, measurement and annotation is preserved verbatim; only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: the descending passage (4146 P″) lands on a floor joint at 1699 CE, and each horizontal pyramid-inch south of it adds one year: 302 to the central axis (2001 CE), 44 to the chamber's north wall (2045 CE), 162 to the well-pit axis (2207 CE).
