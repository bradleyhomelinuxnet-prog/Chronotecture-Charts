A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn charts X, XI and XII (2006), preserved verbatim; only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: the same passages are counted three ways: from the Mayan 5200-year count (X), from the Long-Count start 3114 BC at the entry (XI), and from Year One Anno Mundi at the bottom of the descending passage (XII). All three converge on 1054 AD and 1356 AD.
