Chronotecture 622 AD [4516 AM] — the Islamic Hijrah Calendar, mirrored

A rebuild of Jason M. Breshears’ hand-drawn chart VI·c (Breshears ’07), with every date, span and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its structural key: 622 AD [4516 AM], Mohammed’s flight from Medina, Hijrah Year One. Four spans, 1085, 1111, 1218 and 1228 years, reach an event before it and its counterpart after it; a 552-year bar reaches back to 70 AD, and six bars run forward, the last to 2066 AD, short of 2106 AD [6000 AM].
