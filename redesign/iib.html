Chronotecture The Palindrome of the Great Flood — 1656 years either side of 2239 BCE

A rebuild of Jason M. Breshears’ hand-drawn chart II·b (Breshears ’07), with every date, span and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its structural key: the Great Flood, 2239 BCE. The 1656 years of the Antediluvian World before it mirror the 1656 years of the Postdiluvian World after it, and four spans (792, 1200, 1656, 3000) reach equally far each way. Beneath, the Anunnaki Ner chronology of 7200 years runs from 5239 BCE to 1962 CE in rulers of 600, and the Stonehenge chronoliths run from the 4309 BCE impact in rulers of 414. “Total 7344 yrs. [7200+72+72 years; 7200+144]”.
