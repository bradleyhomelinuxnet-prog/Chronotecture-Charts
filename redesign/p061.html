Chronotecture Anunnaki Chronology — from King David’s Birth (1039 BC) to 1962 AD

A rebuild of Jason M. Breshears’ hand-drawn chart XIX·c, with every date, span and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its structural key: mirrored spans around the birth of King David, 1039 BC [2856 AM] — 318, 326, 368, 649 and 3000 years back to 4039 BC and forward to 1962 AD [5856 AM] — with a lower register of spans leading back to the Great Pyramid, Japheth and Shem.
