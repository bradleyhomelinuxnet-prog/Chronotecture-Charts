Chronotecture Phoenix Chronometry IV — plan of the Upper Chamber & the Chief Cornerstone platform

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn chart “Phoenix Chronometry IV”, a plan (top-down) view of the Great Pyramid, preserved verbatim. Structural key: the Great Step sits on the pyramid's east–west axis and the sarcophagus on its north–south axis, so the plate names the step, sarcophagus and summit platform together as the “central geometric focus of monument”; its sums give 1380 and 5448 = 6000 − 552.
