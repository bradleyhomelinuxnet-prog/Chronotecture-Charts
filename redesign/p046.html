Chronotecture Seth, Al Mamoun and 6000 AM: four readings of the passage conjunct

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn charts XIII–XVI (2006), preserved verbatim; only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: the conjunct of the ascending and descending passages is fixed at 820 AD [4714 AM], the year Al Mamoun broke into the ascending passage. From there 176 P″ up and 1110 P″ out reach 2106 AD = 6000 AM, and the sloping 4146 P″ reaches back to the Lower Chamber level.
