Chronotecture The 6354-year span, 4309 BC to 2046 AD — and the stone the builders rejected

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn summary diagram (signed Breshears 2013; no chart number is written on it). Every statement and figure is preserved verbatim; only the drawing is cleaned up and the long hand-lettered passages are held in numbered pins. Structural key: one great circle whose upper arc spans 6354 years from 4309 BC to 2046 AD, a vertical axis marked 33 AD [3927 AM] running down through the Great Pyramid, and a quadrant for the stone the builders rejected.
