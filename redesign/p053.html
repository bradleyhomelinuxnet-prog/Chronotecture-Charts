Chronotecture 1776 at the Axis Conjunct: 186 short of 6000 and the Anunnaki Ner

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn chart XVII (2006), preserved verbatim; only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: the axis conjunct is 1776 AD [5670 AM], reached three ways: 3036 P″ up from the 1261 BC floor joint, 1110 P″ down from the 666 AD entry, and 1700 − 524 + 600. The monument's 5814 P″ height, 186 short of 6000, mirrors the floor joint's 186 years before the Exodus.
