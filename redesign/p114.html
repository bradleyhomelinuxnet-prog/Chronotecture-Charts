Chronotecture Phoenix Chronometry I — passage sums to 5796 = 1902 AD

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn chart “Phoenix Chronometry I”, preserved verbatim; only the visual language is redrawn. Structural key: passage lengths and heights of the Great Pyramid are added in columns until the totals land on multiples of 138 — 3036, 4968, 6348 — and the entrance-to-bottom chain 2760 + 3036 closes on 5796 = 1902 AD.

Right columns

Uncertain readings
