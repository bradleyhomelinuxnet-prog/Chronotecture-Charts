The Pyramid ChronographThe passages, the plates’ sums, and the Great Step chronometer

The Great Pyramid’s passage system in pyramid inches (P″). The labels are the numbers Breshears wrote on plates 111–117, pinned where he wrote them. The shape uses the plates’ own measures where they give one (the Great Step at 1656, the Gallery 1836, the King’s Chamber 412 × 204 × 230 …) and standard survey figures elsewhere, rounded; the passages are drawn in the centre plane, though on the monument they run about 287 P″ east of it. This renders the Archaix thesis as the plates lay it out; it studies the drawings, not the monument’s purpose.
