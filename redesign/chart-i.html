Chronotecture The Pyramid-Inch Calendar — 3361 P″ + 2435 AM = 5796 AM [1902 AD]

A rebuild of Jason M. Breshears’ hand-drawn chart I (Breshears ’06), with every date, measurement and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its premise: one pyramid-inch (P″) is one year. The two passages meet at 1460 BC [2435 AM]; the Ascendant Passage runs 3361 P″ from there to the face of the Great Step, giving 1902 AD, the start of the Giza Course Countdown Calendar, and the Descendant Passage runs 3036 P″ down to its end joint.
