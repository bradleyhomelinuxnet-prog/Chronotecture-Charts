Chronotecture Sloping and Horizontal Distances in the Great Pyramid

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn survey plate “Sloping and Horizontal Distances in Great Pyramid” (signed Breshears '06; no chart number is written on it — it cites its own “Plate XXX”). Every figure is preserved verbatim in pyramid inches; only the drawing is redrawn. Structural key: horizontal runs are measured out from the central north–south axis (and from the foot of the descending passage), while the sloping figures run along the passages themselves — the outermost, 4554, is written as 1518×3.
