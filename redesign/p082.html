Chronotecture Seven Iron Empire Countdowns — 1836 P″ to 1940–1962 AD

A modern rebuild of Breshears' hand-drawn chart XXVI (signed Breshears '06), preserved verbatim. Its structural key: one fixed length of 1836 P″, added to start years from 104 to 126 AD, lands every countdown in the narrow band 1940–1962 AD on the north end of the Great Step — and 104 and 126 are themselves written as 52+52 and the 126 P″ of the step.
