A rebuild of the Great Step chronology Breshears gives in his text (plates 97 and 99, book pages 121 and 123), with every date and measurement preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. There is no single plate for it: the drawn alignments are on charts XXVII and XXVIII (plates 83–84). The red granite floor begins in 2029 CE.
