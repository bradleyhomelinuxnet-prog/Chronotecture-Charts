Chronotecture Lower Chamber level — vertical and horizontal distances of the descending passage

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn survey plate “Lower Chamber Level — Vertical and Horizontal Distances” (signed Breshears '06; no chart number is written on it). Every measurement, formula and note is preserved verbatim in pyramid inches (P″); only the drawing is cleaned up. Structural key: every run is measured sideways from one of three vertical reference lines — the south wall of the Upper Chamber, the well-pit axis, and the Great Step axis — out to the descending passage floor joint or the west end of the dead-end passage.
