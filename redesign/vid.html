Chronotecture 622 CE [4516 AM] — six spans, each run twice

A rebuild of Jason M. Breshears’ hand-drawn chart VI·d (Breshears ’07), with every date, span and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its structural key: 622 CE [4516 AM], the Islamic Hijrah Calendar. Each bar runs a span from 622 CE to a medieval event, then the same span again to a later one: 622, 632, 692, 711, 726 and 734 years. The ruler is marked in 50-year jubilees and ends at 6000 AM [2106 CE]. “Principle icon of a black meteoric rock in Kaaba.”
