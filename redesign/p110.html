Chronotecture The 7344-year timeline on the pyramid’s sloping corners — 8640, 1080 and 10,800

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn plate on the 7344-year timeline and the 8640 foundation of time (no chart number or signature is written on it). Every figure and annotation is preserved verbatim; only the drawing is cleaned up. Structural key: the pyramid's sloping corners measure 8640 P″ and its face mid-plane slope 7344 P″ — the 7344 years of the internal chronology — and 8640 ÷ 8 faces gives the 1080-year segments marked on the base.
