Chronotecture The Completed Triangle — where the Giza Countdown ends and begins

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn chart IV (2006), with every measurement and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its structural key: the descending passage is counted in years from the 1135 BC [2760 AM] Axis Conjunct — up 1110 P″ to the entry, down 3036 P″ to the floor joint at 1902 AD — and a vertical drop of 1828 P″ closes a triangle reaching 1804 AD, 302 years before 6000 AM.
