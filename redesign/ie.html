Chronotecture The Giza Course Countdown — 204 levels of bricks, 1902 to 2106 AD

A rebuild of Jason M. Breshears’ hand-drawn chart I·e (Breshears ’07), with every date, span and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its structural key: 1902 AD [5796 AM], where the Stonehenge chronolithic system ends. Seven bars run forward from it, from 144 months to 144 years, and the countdown of 204 levels of bricks [years], 2448 months, ends at 6000 Annus Mundi [2106 AD].
