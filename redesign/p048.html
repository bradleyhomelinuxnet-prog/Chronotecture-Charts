Chronotecture Mirror Spans around 1930 AD — Pluto and Ras Shamra to 6000 AM

A rebuild of Jason M. Breshears’ hand-drawn chart XIV·c, with every date, span and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its structural key: every span is mirrored around one axis year, 1930 AD [5824 AM] — 24, 114, 140 and 160 years back to an event, the same distance forward to a prophetic landing.
