Chronotecture Spans from 1135 BC — the comet that darkens the sun

A rebuild of Jason M. Breshears’ hand-drawn chart II·c (Breshears ’07), with every date, span and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its structural key: 1135 BC, “comet darkens the sun”. Eight bars run forward from it, to Strabo’s visit (24 BC) and on to Armageddon at 2106 AD [6000 AM]; three pairs of equal spans, 552, 637 and 1104 years, reach back and forward to a matched pair of events.
