Chronotecture Vertical Distances in the Great Pyramid — heights from apex, base & bedrock

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn survey plate “Vertical Distances in Great Pyramid” (signed Breshears '06; no chart number is written on it). Every measurement, formula and note is preserved verbatim in pyramid inches (P″); only the drawing is cleaned up. Structural key: each vertical is read twice — down from the apex (5814 P″) or the present summit (5448 P″), and up from the base line — so every pair of apex figures differs by the same 366 P″ and lands on a base-height marked lower in the section.
