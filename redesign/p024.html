Cycle mathematics

Every sum written on the plate. Each step adds a passage length in pyramid-inches to the previous date.
990 AM + 666 P″ = 1656 AMSethites begin construction [2905 BCE] + height of entrance above base → Great Flood [2239 BCE]
1656 AM + 1110 P″ = 2766 AMFlood + descending passage to the axis conjunct → [1129 BCE]
2766 AM + 3014 P″ = 5780 AM+ horizontal P″ axis "to axis" → [1886 CE]
1886 CE + 204 = 2090 CEDifferential of 204 P″ to the vertical well-pit axis → return of the Seven Comets, 33rd visitation
All four sums check. The plate's paired dates are mutually consistent (990 AM = 2905 BCE, 1656 AM = 2239 BCE, 2766 AM = 1129 BCE, 5780 AM = 1886 CE). The total path is 666 + 1110 + 3014 + 204 = 4994 P″.
