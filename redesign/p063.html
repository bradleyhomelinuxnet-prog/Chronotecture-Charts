Chronotecture The 583 BC Junction & the Annus Mundi Passage Count

A rebuild of Jason M. Breshears’ hand-drawn plate holding panels XX and XXI, with every date, measurement and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is new. Its structural key: pyramid-inches read as Annus Mundi years — in XXI the measures from the central-axis base (Year One) to the entrance add up to 6000 AM.

Upper chambers and passages: a 1390 P″ horizontal from the 583 BC axis back to the 1298 AD point on the passage, and a 1110 P″ run from the junction to 1693 BC. Hover or tap the numbered markers.
