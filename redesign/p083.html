Chronotecture The Great Step Chronometer — 1902 to 2028 in Thirds of 126

A modern rebuild of Breshears' hand-drawn chart XXVII (signed Breshears '06), preserved verbatim. Its structural key: the 126 P″ top of the Great Step is a calendar from 1902 to 2028, ruled every 12 years and split into thirds of 42 (1944, 1986, 2028); the verticals to the lower chamber and Middle chambers fall on it at 1945 and 2005.
