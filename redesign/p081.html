Chronotecture The Measured Section — Great Step to the South Wall of the King's Chamber

A modern rebuild of Breshears' hand-drawn chart XXV (signed Breshears '06): the dimensioned survey behind the whole King's Chamber series, preserved verbatim. Its structural key: every distance is measured south from the face of the Great Step — 204 P″ to the Well-Pit axis, 330 to the King's Chamber, 534 to its south wall — and the chains of dimensions add up (204+330 = 534, 126+204 = 330, 270+204 = 474).
