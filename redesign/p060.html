Chronotecture From the Pyramid’s completion — span-bars out of 2815 BC [1080 AM]

A rebuild of Breshears' hand-drawn chart XIX·b, every date, span, formula and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: every bar starts at 2815 BC [1080 AM], “Great Pyramid complete” and runs to a dated landing — the Flood, 583 BC, the Temple, 745 BC, 1825 BC, 1986 and 2046 AD — while two 1656-year (414×4) arcs frame the antediluvian and ancient worlds.
