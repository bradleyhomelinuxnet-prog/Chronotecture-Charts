Chronotecture Spans converging on the 1054 AD supernova in Taurus

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn chart X·b (2007). Every date, span and annotation is kept verbatim; only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: 1054 AD [4948 AM], the supernova in Taurus and the split of Roman papal authority, is the arrowhead. Bars of 3000, 2500, 1600 and 432 years end on it, and 744 and 1052 years run on from it to 1798 and 2106 AD.
