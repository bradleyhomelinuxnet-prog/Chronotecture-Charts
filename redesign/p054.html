Chronotecture The 1776 Mirror — spans converging on the birth of the United States

A rebuild of Breshears' hand-drawn plate holding charts XVII·b and XVII·c, every date, span and annotation preserved verbatim; only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: 1776 AD [5670 AM] is the anchor — in XVII·b every span-bar lands on it from the past, and in XVII·c each span is mirrored, the same number of years before and after 1776, out to 2106 AD [6000 AM].
