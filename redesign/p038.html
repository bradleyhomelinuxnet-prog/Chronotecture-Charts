Chronotecture The Entrance at 2590 BC, the Mayan Long-Count and the 2070 AD Well-Pit Axis

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn chart VIII ('06), preserved verbatim (chamber names follow the atlas's Upper / Middle / Lower convention); only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: the entrance is dated 2590 BC — 524 P″ back to 3114 BC starts the Mayan Long-Count, while 1110 + 3036 + 346 + 168 P″ forward run through 1480 BC, 1556 and 1902 to 2070 AD.
