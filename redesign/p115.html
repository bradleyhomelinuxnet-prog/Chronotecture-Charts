Chronotecture Phoenix Chronometry II — the lower chamber in multiples of 138

A modern rebuild of Jason M. Breshears' hand-drawn chart “Phoenix Chronometry II”, a close-up of the lower chamber beneath the Great Pyramid, preserved verbatim. Structural key: two depths below the base (1070 to the chamber, 1162 to the passage) are each built up by column addition into a boxed total — 3036 (138×22) and 2760 — with 552 and 138 boxed inside the chamber itself.
