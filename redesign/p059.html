Chronotecture Passages as calendars — Year One to the Great Step, and the foundation of 2815 BCE

A rebuild of Breshears' hand-drawn plate carrying charts XVIII and XIX, two Great Pyramid passage sections; every measurement, date and annotation is preserved verbatim and only the visual language is redesigned. Its structural key: pyramid-inches are read as years — in XVIII counted back from the Upper Chamber (1 AM) through the Great Step (534 AM) to the axis conjunct (3894/3895 AM); in XIX counted up from the base foundation (2815 BCE [1080 AM]) through the 666 P″ entrance to the conjunct at 1039 BCE.
